How it works

Three steps, no window to open, nothing to click.

1
⌥

Hold the key

Right ⌥ by default, anywhere — an email, a chat, a terminal. A small panel appears at your cursor to show it's listening.

2
🗣️

Say what you mean

Words show up live as it hears them. Double-tap instead to keep recording for a longer thought.

3
✓

Let go

The finished text is typed where your cursor was. Esc cancels. If one ever goes astray, it's one click away under the menu bar icon.

What makes it different

Dictation that cleans up the way people actually talk.

💡

It understands what you meant

Removes “um”, resolves “3 — sorry, 4”, and works out ride from write using the rest of the sentence.

📝

It gives text its shape

A spoken list becomes a list. A dictated email gets its greeting and sign-off. A new topic starts a new paragraph.

🧠

It learns your words

Names and jargon trip up every dictation app. Teach it once and it's fixed everywhere — and nothing is ever learned behind your back.

🛡️

It won't invent things

Every edit is checked against what you actually said. Anything that looks made up is thrown away in favour of the original.

💸

It shows what it costs

Bring your own API key from Anthropic or OpenAI. Spending is tracked to the tenth of a cent, and short phrases skip the AI entirely.

🔄

It updates itself

When a new version is out, it asks. One click downloads it, verifies it was signed by the same developer, and restarts.

🔒

Your voice stays on your Mac

Transcription runs entirely on-device and audio is never uploaded — not to me, not to anyone. The optional cleanup step sends only the transcript to the AI provider you choose. No accounts, no telemetry, nothing collected, and the microphone is only open while you're holding the key.

Installing

A minute, once. After that it keeps itself up to date.

  1. DownloadThe .dmg from the button above.
  2. DragDouble-click it and drag Murmur to Applications.
  3. OpenIt lives in your menu bar, not the Dock. One click — no security warnings.
  4. AllowGrant the three permissions macOS asks for. It walks you through them.

Signed and notarised by Apple, so it opens with a single click. Requires macOS 26 or later on Apple Silicon.
