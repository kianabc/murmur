Murmur

Talk instead of typing. Anywhere on your Mac.

Download for macOS

Free and open source · Requires macOS 26 or later on Apple Silicon

What you say

“I'm trying to, um, write — sorry, ride my motorcycle to work.”

↓
What gets typed

I'm trying to ride my motorcycle to work.

How it works

  1. Hold right ⌘ anywhere — an email, a chat, a code editor.
  2. Say what you mean. A small panel shows the words as it hears them.
  3. Let go. The finished text appears at your cursor.

Double-tap the key to keep recording for longer thoughts. Esc cancels.

What makes it different

It understands what you meant

Removes “um”, resolves “3 — sorry, 4”, and works out ride from write using the rest of the sentence.

It learns your words

Names and jargon trip up every dictation app. Teach it once and it's fixed everywhere.

It won't invent things

Every edit is checked against what you actually said. Anything that looks made up is thrown away.

It shows what it costs

Bring your own API key from Anthropic or OpenAI. Spending is tracked to the tenth of a cent.

Your voice stays on your Mac

Transcription runs entirely on-device. Audio is never uploaded — not to me, not to anyone.

The optional cleanup step sends the transcript to the AI provider you choose, and only then. No accounts, no telemetry, nothing collected. Turn it off and Murmur still works.

Installing

  1. Download the .dmg above.
  2. Double-click it and drag Murmur to your Applications folder.
  3. Open it. Murmur lives in your menu bar, not the Dock.
  4. Grant the permissions macOS asks for — a one-time step it walks you through.

Signed and notarised by Apple, so it opens with a single click. No security warnings to work around.
